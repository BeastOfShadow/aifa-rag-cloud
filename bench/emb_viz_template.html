    Embedder comparison

    Same-class neighbors: for each document I take the 10 most similar ones, leaving out the other document of the same drug, and count how many share its first-level ATC class. The higher it is, the more the model places drugs with the same use close together.

    Nearest neighbors: they are computed in the model's original space, not on the map, so a neighbor can look far away in the projection. They are the documents that retrieval considers closest.

    Right chunks in s5: over 20 questions about specific drugs, how many of the 6 chunks kept after reranking come from the drug asked about.
